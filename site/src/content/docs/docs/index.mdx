r3cui replaces the touchscreen UI on the [Unfolded Circle Remote 3](https://www.unfoldedcircle.com/) with a simple,
single-purpose remote for a home theater. It shows what's playing in Kodi, your movie and TV library as a wall of
posters, your receiver's inputs and the volume, and lights the buttons to match the input you're on.

It runs on the remote through the custom UI slot that Unfolded Circle provides for exactly this. It's written in Rust
with [Slint](https://slint.rs).

:::caution[Beta software]
Things may break, and things are 100% bound to change. r3cui works closely with [Kodi](https://kodi.tv) and
[Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io) and isn't plug and play yet: you'll need to clone the project and
modify it to suit your home theater configuration and setup.
:::
