---
title: Kodi integration
description: How r3cui follows Kodi's library and playback, and controls audio tracks and subtitles.
---

r3cui ties deep into Kodi. It uses Kodi's own library data to give you a second screen that follows library
navigation and media playback.

## Library wall

When Kodi shows its Movies or TV list, the remote mirrors it as a wall of posters, in the same order as Kodi. Move
through the list with the d-pad: the highlight on the remote moves with each press, and the title, year, rating and
plot of the highlighted item show below the wall.

## Now playing

When a film or episode plays, the remote shows its poster, the time left and when it ends, and the playback controls.

## Audio tracks and subtitles

Tap **Audio** or **Subtitles** to choose a track by its real name, as Kodi lists it.
